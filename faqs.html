FAQS

Everything you need to know about HACKWAVE 2.0.

Who can participate? +
Anyone with a passion for building! Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned pro, if you love tech and solving problems, you're welcome.
Is it free? +
Absolutely! Participation in HackWave 2.0 is completely free of charge.
Do I need prior experience? +
No prior hackathon experience is required. We will have mentors available to guide you throughout the event.
What should I bring? +
Bring your laptop, chargers, any necessary hardware, a valid ID card, and lots of enthusiasm!
Will accommodation be provided? +
Since it's an offline 36-hour event, designated resting areas will be provided on campus.
Can I participate solo? +
We highly encourage participating in a team of 2-4 members to build better projects, but solo participation is also allowed.
